NeuroTrack wearable head impact monitor

Spring 2026 | 1st place, BU BMES Design-A-Thon (sponsored by BTEC)

Hand sectioning at multiple angles is hard to repeat. On a five-person team I lead machining, prototyping and organization for a device that makes multi-angle histological sectioning consistent.

Multi-robot autonomous navigation

Fall 2025 | 12 robots in a 9 m x 5 m motion capture arena
  1. Motion capture
  2. A* with Dubins curves
  3. 1.5 s prediction
  4. Multi-radius avoidance

The planning was the easy part. The learning came from debugging: a coordinate-frame mismatch, gridlock from overlapping detection radii, and finished robots blocking paths. A graduated collision radius and filtered obstacle lists fixed them.

Root causes traced from data, then fixed in the planner.

A single servo arm grew into two arms with interchangeable grippers. I moved control from one Arduino to a wireless ESP32 network and tested load capacity and positional feedback.
